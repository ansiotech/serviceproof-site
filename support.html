Help for the workday.

ServiceProof helps you keep a clear, shareable record after a job is complete.

Common questions

Where are my report photos and PDFs?

They are stored locally on the iPhone in V1. Deleting a report removes the files the app owns for that report.

When is a Report Credit used?

One credit is used only after a formal report version finishes finalizing. Drafts, previews, downloads, shares, and deletion do not consume another credit.

Can I sign in with email and Apple?

You may connect eligible sign-in methods to the same ServiceProof account from Settings. Business contact email is separate from your sign-in email.

Why is a report unavailable?

Check the device connection, then try again. If the report file was removed or the account is unavailable, the app will explain the next available action.
