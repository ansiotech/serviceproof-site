Description

ServiceProof gives independent service professionals a clear way to document completed work. Add before-and-after photos, service notes, customer confirmation, a star rating, and a signature. Then finalize a polished PDF report that is easy to share.

Built for mobile car detailers, residential cleaners, and handymen, ServiceProof keeps the workflow focused from job completion through customer follow-up.

  • Capture before-and-after service photos
  • Use focused industry templates
  • Collect customer confirmation, rating, and signature
  • Create a professional PDF service report
  • Keep photos, signatures, and PDFs locally on your device in V1
