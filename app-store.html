ServiceProof

Clear service records, right from the field.

ServiceProof helps independent service professionals capture completed work, review the result with a customer, and share a polished service report from iPhone or iPad.

Built for the handoff

Start with the customer and service details, then add before-and-after photos, damage records, work notes, and an industry checklist. At the handoff, the customer can review the result, choose a rating, and sign the record.

When you finalize, ServiceProof creates a formal PDF report that you can open or share through the iOS share sheet.

Three focused workflows

  • Mobile Car Detailing: document vehicle condition, detailing work, and the finished result.
  • Residential Cleaning: record room-level work and the condition left for the customer.
  • Handyman: capture repair observations, completed work, and confirmation at handoff.

Report Credits

A Report Credit is used only when a formal report version successfully finalizes. Drafting, reviewing, downloading, sharing, deleting, and re-rendering the same version do not use another Credit.

Report Credits are consumable in-app purchases. There are no subscriptions, recurring charges, cash-out, or transfers. Apple presents the final price and any applicable tax before purchase.

Your files stay with you

In V1, service reports, photos, signatures, and PDFs stay on your iPhone or iPad unless you choose to share them. ServiceProof does not enable iCloud synchronization or automatic report-file uploads.
